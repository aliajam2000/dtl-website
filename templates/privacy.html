This notice describes the current website and the intended application workflow. Partner applications and participant registration are not currently open.

The current public website

No application information is submitted while the form is disabled. The website does not use advertising trackers or analytics, and does not store form drafts in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the public pages and may process technical request information under its own policies.

When applications open

The form is designed to request your name, professional email, organization, country, operating region, languages, experience, proposed collaboration, and message. An optional website link can be provided. Do not include sensitive information or personal details about community members.

The intended purpose is to assess potential research collaboration and communicate with applicants. Applications will be held separately from household research records and restricted to authorized reviewers. Applications will not be published.

Supabase is the intended storage and backend provider. Cloudflare Turnstile is the planned abuse-prevention provider; when enabled, it may process technical information needed to validate a request. No Turnstile script is loaded while this form is disabled.

Before accepting applications

DTL must publish a verified privacy contact, identify the responsible data controller and applicable rights process, confirm storage location and any transfer arrangements, and specify a retention and deletion schedule. These details are not yet finalized, so collection remains closed.

Household research is separate

Partnership enquiries do not authorize participant data collection. Any future household study will require its own purpose-specific information, appropriate consent process, access rules, retention plan, and local review before fieldwork.

Names and identifiers may be retained in restricted operational records when necessary for authorized follow-up. Public reporting is intended to use aggregate or appropriately de-identified information after a disclosure-risk review.

Questions about participation

Contact DTL at aliajam9731@gmail.com with questions about collaboration, this website, or responsible data handling. No participant records can be submitted through this website. Please do not send household members' personal information by email or through the public code repository.
