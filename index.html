Our approach

Start with understanding.

Our first priority is to establish a reliable, community-centered poverty data system before selecting interventions. Household knowledge, repeated measurement, and rigorous evaluation are essential. Identifying a common need does not by itself establish that a particular solution will work.

The 1M Project

First understand 100. Then learn how to scale.

Our long-term mission is to help at least one million people sustainably escape severe poverty. The First 100 is our first research and operational milestone—not a claim that 100 people have already been helped.
