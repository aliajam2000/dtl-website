A strong partnership begins by understanding each other’s priorities.

Potential collaborators include local NGOs, community-based organizations, universities, research groups, field enumerators, and development practitioners.

Collaboration may involve shaping research questions, adapting assessment tools, supporting informed consent, conducting approved fieldwork, or interpreting findings together.

DTL is an early-stage initiative. An application does not guarantee funding, employment, a contract, or field deployment. Scope, resources, and responsibilities must be agreed before any work begins.
