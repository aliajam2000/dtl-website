About Development Technology Lab

A long-term mission.
An honest starting point.

DTL is an early-stage, research-driven initiative exploring how technology, local knowledge, and rigorous evaluation can help reduce severe poverty.

Our mission

Lasting improvements
in people’s lives.

Help at least 1,000,000 people sustainably escape extreme or severe poverty.

This is a long-term mission, not a statement of impact already achieved. To work toward it responsibly, we first need to understand the people, places, and constraints that any intervention would address.

We aim to connect research with practical, scalable, financially sustainable solutions—and to evaluate whether those solutions actually help.

Milestone 001

The First 100.

Our initial research and validation milestone focuses on building the foundations to work with the first 100 people: trusted partnerships, appropriate consent, reliable baselines, and meaningful follow-up.

Target, not achieved reach or verified impact.
