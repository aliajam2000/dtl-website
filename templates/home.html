01 / The starting point

Poverty is complex.
Our starting point
is simple: listen.

Behind every poverty measure is a person, a household, and a local context.

Income matters. So do unstable work, access to services, health, and the constraints people face in their communities. A solution that works in one place may not work in another.

Before choosing what to build, we want to understand what holds people back—and what they believe could help.

The 1M Project / Milestone 001

Think long term.
Start with the first 100.

Our long-term mission is to help at least 1,000,000 people sustainably escape extreme or severe poverty. Our first step is to build the understanding and research foundations to work responsibly with the first 100.

A target we are working toward. Not an achieved result.
