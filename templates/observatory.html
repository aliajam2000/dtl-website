Infrastructure in development

Poverty is not static.

The DTL Poverty Observatory is a planned longitudinal research infrastructure for understanding households, their constraints, and changes in welfare over time.

What we are building

A connected
research record.

We are preparing the foundations for consent-based field research. Field deployment and real-data collection are not yet open.

01

People & households

Register consenting participants, connect individuals to households, and document informed consent.

02

Conditions & needs

Establish baselines, record reported needs, and understand economic and community-level constraints.

03

Follow-up & learning

Repeat measurements, document interventions, and examine changes in welfare to support future evaluation.

Responsible by design

People first.
Their information, protected.

Informed participation

People should understand what is collected, why, who may access it, and how to raise a concern or withdraw where applicable. Consent must be understandable in the local context.

Restricted operational records

Names and identifiers may be needed for authorized follow-up. They belong in restricted records, with access limited by role and assignment.

Careful public reporting

Public findings should use aggregated or appropriately de-identified information, with disclosure risk checked before release.
